# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Locals in and around Mar del Plata, Argentina, browsing on their phones, looking
for a distinctive handmade gift or a piece of decor: soy candles and resin
accessories made in short, one-off runs. They arrive from Instagram, a Google
search ("velas en mar del plata"), or word of mouth. The job to be done is
"find something I love and buy it without friction" — the purchase itself
finishes over WhatsApp, so the site's job is to make someone want the piece and
trust the maker before they tap through.

## Product Purpose

Lumika Objetos is the storefront and brand home for a small handmade-goods
business. It exists to show the work at its best, carry the brand's voice, rank
for local intent ("velas / decoración en Mar del Plata"), and hand a warm,
ready-to-buy lead to WhatsApp. Success: the page makes the craft feel desirable
and the maker feel real, then routes the sale out cleanly. It is not a checkout;
it is a window and a handshake.

## Brand Personality

Playful, handmade, bold. Voice is warm and direct, first-person-maker, never
corporate. Confident saturated color (hot pink, electric blue) on warm cream,
oversized display type, a hand-drawn wavy underline — the personality of someone
who makes things by hand and is proud of them. Evokes delight and warmth, not
luxury restraint or minimal cool.

## Anti-references

- Mass-produced marketplace template (generic Shopify/Etsy grid with no voice).
- Cold luxury minimalism: thin gray type on white, lots of negative space, hushed
  premium tone. Wrong emotional register for handmade-and-friendly.
- Corporate SaaS polish: hero-metric blocks, identical icon-card grids, tracked
  uppercase eyebrows on every section.

## Design Principles

- **Show the work.** Photography and real product carry the page; chrome stays out
  of the way. The candles are the hero, not the UI.
- **Sound like a person.** Copy is the maker talking, in Rioplatense Spanish, with
  warmth and zero buzzwords.
- **Confident, not loud-for-its-own-sake.** Big type and saturated color are
  deliberate brand, applied with rhythm — committed, but never noisy.
- **Frictionless to buy.** Every path leads cleanly to WhatsApp; never make someone
  hunt for how to purchase.
- **Local and findable.** The site earns its place in local search and reads as
  unmistakably Mar del Plata.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text and any text on the saturated pink/blue or cream
backgrounds must clear 4.5:1 (3:1 for large display type). Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion` for the marquee and all transitions. Mobile-first: real
touch targets (≥44px), readable type (≥14px), no horizontal scroll. Spanish
(es-AR) as the primary language.
